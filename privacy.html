Privacy

Privacy statement

What this website does and does not collect.

This site collects nothing about you

There is no analytics, no tracking, no advertising and no cookies. We do not set any cookie, and nothing you do here is recorded or sent to us.

The search and filter boxes on the people and projects pages run entirely in your browser. What you type is never sent anywhere.

There are no accounts, no sign-in and no forms to submit.

What other parties necessarily see

  • Hosting. The site is served by GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub receives your IP address and request details in order to deliver the page, and handles them under GitHub's privacy statement.
  • One third-party script. Each page loads a JavaScript library (js-yaml) from the cdnjs content delivery network, operated by Cloudflare. Your browser requests that file directly, so Cloudflare sees the request.
  • Links out. Pages link to ORCID, DOI resolvers, the Maastricht University CRIS portal, publishers and similar sites. Those are ordinary links: nothing is shared until you follow one, and the destination's own policy then applies.

Personal data published here

The site publishes information about members of the group: name, role, research interests, a photograph, and links to public professional profiles such as ORCID, the university CRIS portal and, where the person has provided one, GitHub, Google Scholar or LinkedIn. It also lists publications, which are drawn from public records.

This is published with the knowledge of the people concerned. If you are listed here and want an entry corrected or removed, write to info-ids@maastrichtuniversity.nl and we will act on it.

Who is responsible

The site is run by the Health AI Research Group at the Institute of Data Science, Department of Advanced Computing Sciences, Maastricht University. Maastricht University is the data controller, and its institutional privacy statement governs anything this page does not cover, including how to exercise your rights under the GDPR and how to reach the university's data protection officer.
